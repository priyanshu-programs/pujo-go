- How It Works phone fan — conveyor sweep (done 2026-09-29, see `updates.md`; every knob lives in the `SWEEP` block in `HowItWorks.tsx`).
  - Since 2026-09-30 the pose is applied as an `x`/`y` transform on a full-size **slot** wrapper (`absolute inset-0`), not as CSS `left`/`top` — animating layout properties inside the `maskImage` group was the judder. `PHONE_FAN`'s numbers are unchanged and still read as container-relative percentages, because a slot the size of the container makes a percentage transform equivalent to the old `left`/`top`. Keep it that way: do NOT reintroduce animated `left`/`top`, and do not convert the poses to pixels (that needs a measurement, which breaks SSR/first paint).
  - `SWEEP.enterX`/`exitX` belong on the inner card, where they stay element-relative. `zIndex` must stay inside the animated object with `{duration: 0, delay: settleDelay}`, or the planes pop while the fan is at rest.
  - Still outstanding: the sweep is one-directional. `select()` bumps the outgoing step's generation regardless of direction, so "previous" replays the next-direction choreography (one survivor moves backwards, another crosses two planes). Fix would be to make the direction drive which slot the new card is dealt into.
  - The fan is a CONVEYOR, not a carousel: on a step change the front phone sweeps out to the left and is destroyed, the other two glide left into the gap, and a brand-new phone is dealt in from the right so three frames are always on screen. A swept phone never comes back — its card is unmounted via a per-step generation counter in the React key.
  - Nothing overshoots. `DHAK`/`easeDhak` is deliberately NOT used here: it peaks past its target, which reads as a bounce on frames this large. Use `EASE_GLIDE` (easeOutQuint) for anything staying, `EASE_SWEEP` (ease-in) for the exit.
  - The exit holds full opacity briefly, then dissolves mid-flight — the phone is never seen stopping, and nothing clips this composition horizontally, so that fade is also what hides it from the step copy in the left column.
